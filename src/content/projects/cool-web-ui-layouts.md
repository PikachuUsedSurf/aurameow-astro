Not every repository needs a mission statement. This one is exactly what
its own description says: goofing around trying sleek styles and seeing
what sticks — no client, no deadline, no spec.

It's the kind of low-stakes practice that doesn't show up on a resume line
item but shows up everywhere else — in knowing what a good hover state
feels like, or how much whitespace a headline actually needs before it
looks intentional rather than empty.
